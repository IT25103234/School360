Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions regarding support, logins, and campus assistance.

How long does it take to receive a response?

➕
Our typical response window is within 2 to 4 business hours for portal users and support tickets. General inquiries submitted via this form are answered within 24 hours.

How do enrolled students submit priority technical tickets?

➕
Logged-in students can access their Student Portal dashboard and navigate to the Support tab to open a direct ticket with attachment uploads and live status tracking.

Where can prospective students inquire about new admissions?

➕
Prospective students can select 'Admissions & Course Enrollment' under the inquiry subject dropdown on this page, or contact the admissions helpline directly at +94 11 236 0361.

What should I do if I forget my login password or digital QR code?

➕
You can contact the IT Support Desk or request a credential reset from an Enrollment Officer or Administrator. Alternatively, submit a message here specifying your username and full name.
